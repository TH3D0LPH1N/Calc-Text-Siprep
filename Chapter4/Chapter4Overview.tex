\textbf{\underline{\large{Chapter 4 Overview: }}} \par

There are two main contexts for derivatives: graphing and motion. In this chapter, we will consider both these applications of the derivative. Much of this is a review of material covered in precalculus. Key topics include: \begin{itemize}
    \item Extremes
    \item The First and Second Derivative Tests
    \item Optimization
    \item Derivative-related theorems
    \item Intervals of increasing and decreasing
    \item Advanced graphing using the derivative
    \item Rectilinear motion
\end{itemize}

This chapter makes up a solid portion of the multiple choice, along with also being at least one full free response question (and often parts of others) on the AP exam.
